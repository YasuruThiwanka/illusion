About The Event

The Art Society of the Faculty of Technology, University of Colombo proudly presents Illusion, a dynamic musical event celebrating creativity, rhythm, and student talent. This event provides an excellent platform for brands to engage with a youthful, energetic audience while supporting arts and culture within the university.

  • Audience Reach: 2,000+ in-person attendees; 20,000+ digital reach
  • Entry: Free for all students
  • Estimated Budget: LKR 2,000,000.00
